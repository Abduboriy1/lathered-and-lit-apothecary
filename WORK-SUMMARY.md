## What Was Done

### 1. Rebrand to "Lathered & Lit Apothecary" (Sep 5)

- Replaced every "Lathered & Lit Soap Co." reference with **Lathered & Lit Apothecary** across the site, the browser tab title, and the footer copyright ("© 2026 Lathered & Lit Apothecary LLC").
- Built a new wordmark that mirrors the look of your Facebook posts: **LATHERED & LIT** in serif caps with spaced-out **APOTHECARY** beneath, flanked by two small accent dots. There is a stacked version for the hero and footer and a single-line version for the top menu.
- New heart-style tab icon matching the header artwork.
- Footer redesigned into a taller, modern layout with a brand column, links, and social icons pointing to the Lathered & Lit Apothecary Facebook page and Instagram.
- Cleared out the old, unused logo and hero images.

### 2. Design Refresh (Sep 5)

- Reviewed every section against the brand colors (teal, blush, rose-gold, gold) and tightened the colors, spacing, and lettering so the site reads as polished and premium.
- New hero: your updated hero artwork was cleaned up and placed properly, the "floating" header image was fixed, and the brand name now sits centered above the message and image at a larger size.
- Hero message block: larger text to fill the empty space, and the three promise lines ("Free shipping on orders over $50 · 100% natural, skin-loving ingredients · Handmade on our family farm") now sit on a single line.
- Created a reusable **hand-drawn leaf vine** border, drawn from the leaves in your hero image. It runs along the footer edge, and its wavy stem now shapes the top edge of the footer so the section follows the curve.
- Extended the "What Our Customers Say" background down to the footer to remove the mismatched gap.
- Softer, smoother fade-in effects on sections and product cards as you scroll.
- Restored the top menu bar after trying an alternate look you preferred not to keep.

### 3. Speed Improvements (Sep 5)

- Rebuilt the site's images in a lighter format. The images now load about **60x smaller** than before, which makes the site noticeably faster, especially on phones and slower connections.
- The hero image is given loading priority so the first screen appears quickly.
- Repeat visits now load faster because the site is remembered by the visitor's browser.
- Added the Google-search essentials: a search description, a proper share preview (branded image, title, and text when the site is shared on Facebook, Instagram, or by text message), and the files search engines use to index the site correctly.

### 4. Launch on Your Own Domain (Sep 5)

- Put the site on a fast, secure hosting platform and resolved the issues that were blocking the first launch.
- Set up a one-step publishing process so future updates can go live quickly.
- Walked through connecting **latheredandlitapothecary.com** so the site serves from your own domain instead of a temporary address.

### 5. Mobile Cleanup (Sep 5)

- Reviewed every page at phone size and fixed the layout issues throughout.
- Added a proper mobile menu: an animated hamburger button that turns into an X, a slide-down menu with Home / Shop / Your Bag, a cart count badge, social links, and taps outside the menu to close it.
- Tuned the header, hero, product grid, product pages, cart, testimonials, and footer for small screens.

### 6. Marketing Tools & Your Feedback (Sep 8)

- **Cookie consent banner:** a "We use cookies" notice with Accept / Decline, remembered per visitor. Tracking only turns on after a visitor accepts, which keeps you on the right side of privacy rules.
- **Mailchimp newsletter signup:** the footer signup form goes straight into your Mailchimp list, with clear "you're subscribed" / "already subscribed" / "something went wrong" messages. Added a tasteful welcome popup that appears once, remembers people who sign up, and stays hidden for 30 days after it is dismissed.
- **Facebook & Instagram ad tracking (Meta Pixel):** installed and ready. Once switched on it tells Meta when someone views the site, views a product, adds to cart, starts checkout, or joins your newsletter — which is what lets you run ads, retarget visitors, and see which ads actually make sales. It waits for cookie consent before it records anything, and nothing is lost in the meantime. **One short setup step is still needed from you — see the next section.**
- Logo fix: removed the fade on "LIT" so the whole word is one solid color.
- Your requested tweaks: hero image hidden on mobile, "Apothecary" made larger under the logo, footer centered on mobile, the three badges (Natural Ingredients · Made with Love · Gentle & Nourishing) on one line on mobile, and testimonials adjusted to a realistic mix of 4- and 5-star reviews.

### 7. New Email, New Online Store & Product System (Sep 9)

**New business email**

- The business now runs on **admin@latheredandlitapothecary.com**. This is the address behind the new store account, and it should become your contact address for Mailchimp and Facebook, replacing the old Admin@latheredandlit.com.

**New online store**

- Created and verified a fresh Shopify account under the new email, with a new store to replace the old Lathered & Lit Soap Co. one, and connected the website to it.
- Set up the behind-the-scenes access that lets your products, images, and stock levels be managed for you.
- Turned on stock tracking so the site shows real availability instead of "Out of Stock".
- Added six sample products (bar soap, body butter, bath bomb) with placeholder labels, prices, descriptions, and stock of 25 each. These are placeholders — rename them, re-price them, or delete them.
